Step 01 · Free head start

Redeem the four official codes

Before your first fight, open the blue tick / checkmark on the left side of the screen and redeem the four codes printed on the official page: REBALANCEAGAIN, HAVEFUN, NEWCURRENCY, and HEADSTART. Do it from the lobby — the game does not pause while the codes menu is open. Full steps on the codes page.

Step 02 · Safe farming

Farm food during spawn immunity

You spawn as a small circular tank with a single turret, and new spawns get a short immunity window. Move with WASD and shoot the colorful shapes — squares, triangles, pentagons — for XP while nobody can hurt you. Treat "destroy 100 of each shape" as your first concrete goals; they are also badge objectives.

Step 03 · Early stats

Spend your first stat points (E)

Press E to open the stats upgrade menu. Beginner guides recommend bullet damage and bullet health first — once immunity ends, stronger bullets pierce weaker enemy fire — then max HP or HP regen. The full priority breakdown is on the stats page.

Step 04 · First evolution

Pick your first class (Q)

Press Q to open the tank upgrade menu when a class upgrade becomes available. Later upgrades add turrets or change your shots — flame, freeze, and more — and paths can lock each other out, so check the in-game Index before committing. Read more on the tanks page.

Step 05 · First gems

Hunt tanks — when it is safe

The official description says destroying tanks earns gems. Once your bullets reliably win trades, start picking fights; until then, keep farming. Dying is normal — sessions are short — so rebuild during the next immunity window instead of forcing 1v1s. More on the gems page.

Beginner FAQ

First-life questions.

Quick answers that come up during the checklist.

What are the controls?

Guides report WASD to move, E to open the stats upgrade menu, and Q to open the tank upgrade menu. Your tank shoots at your aim — practice leading shapes during immunity.

How long does spawn immunity last?

Guides confirm a short window but no official duration is published. Treat it as seconds, not minutes: farm immediately and stay alert when it ends.

Should I fight other players right away?

Not until your bullets win trades. Omnidirectional farm builds lose aimed duels; if PVP is the goal, frontal-barrel paths are the reliable pick. See the tanks page.

Is there anything worth buying with Robux?

No. The public game-pass catalog showed only one offsale pass named hello — there is no priced pass to consider. Details on the gems page.
