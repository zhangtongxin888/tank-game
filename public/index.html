The core loop

Four moves every life runs on.

Deaths are part of the game — trackers show short average sessions. Each respawn, this loop is your rebuild plan.

1
🟧

Shoot food for XP

You spawn as a small circular tank with one turret. Shoot squares, triangles, and pentagons to gain levels — food is also your badge track.

2
📊

Spend stat points (E)

Press E for the stats upgrade menu. Official page names damage, health, and speed; guides confirm a wider menu including regen and bullet stats.

3
🔀

Upgrade class (Q)

Press Q for the tank upgrade menu and evolve along a build path. Check the in-game Index before committing — paths can lock each other out.

4
💎

Destroy tanks for gems

The official description is blunt: destroy tanks and earn gems. Fight when your bullets win trades, farm when they do not.

Progression

One life, four phases.

Every run through the arena follows the same arc. Knowing which phase you are in tells you what to do next.

PHASE 01

Immunity farm

New spawns get a short immunity window. Use it to shoot food and stack early levels before other tanks can touch you.

PHASE 02

Stat stacking

Open E and put early points into bullet damage and bullet health so your shots win trades, then max HP or regen.

PHASE 03

Class commits

Open Q at upgrade levels and evolve. The in-game Index shows legal build paths — consult it before locking a branch.

PHASE 04

Gem hunts

Once your build wins fights, destroy other tanks for gems and chase the 10,000-shape badges on the side.

Common mistakes

Four ways new tanks get scrapped.

Most early deaths and wasted hours come from the same habits. Dodge these and every life goes further.

✕

Redeeming codes mid-fight

The game does not pause while the codes menu is open — redeem in the lobby, or other tanks can shoot you while you type.

✕

Ignoring bullet health

After spawn immunity, weaker bullets lose every trade. Early guides push bullet damage and bullet health before anything else.

✕

Picking farm barrels for PVP

Omnidirectional tanks with side and rear barrels farm well but their DPS cannot be aimed — frontal-barrel tanks are the reliable PVP pick.

✕

Trusting 30-code dumps

Long third-party code lists lag the official page badly. Only the four strings printed on the game page are confirmed current.
