Strategy

How rare are the big ones?

Award counts at our August 20, 2026 check tell the story: starter badges are everywhere, the 10,000s are a flex.

🟧

100-shape badges: common

Tens of millions of players have earned the starter badges. You will pick them up naturally in your first sessions just by farming food.

🏆

10,000-shape badges: rare

Award counts drop to the low hundreds of thousands — the 10,000s are a genuine long-term goal, not a weekend project.

🎯

Food is never wasted

Shapes give XP, fund your stats, and tick badge counters at the same time. Even PVP-focused lives should farm between fights.

Beginner tip: make "100 of each shape" your first session goal alongside the five-step route — it teaches the map, the controls, and the food economy in one pass.

Badges FAQ

Badge hunting questions.

Quick answers for collectors.

How many badges does Tank Game have?

Six, per the official badges catalog checked August 20, 2026: a 100-destroy and a 10,000-destroy badge for each of squares, triangles, and pentagons.

Do badges give anything besides pride?

Badges are Roblox achievements — their real value here is structure: they turn food farming into clear goals while you level stats and classes.

What is the fastest way to farm shapes?

Farm during spawn immunity when possible, invest in bullet damage early so shapes break faster, and use quiet map areas until your build wins trades. See the stats page.

Are there other food shapes besides the three?

The badge list proves at least squares, triangles, and pentagons. Anything beyond that is not officially documented — the in-game arena is the reference.
