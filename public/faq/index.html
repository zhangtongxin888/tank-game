Gameplay

Playing the arena.

Deeper answers live on the linked system pages.

How do I level up?

Shoot food shapes — squares, triangles, pentagons — for XP, then spend stat points with E and evolve your tank with Q. The beginner guide walks the full first session.

What are the controls?

Guides report WASD to move, E for the stats upgrade menu, and Q for the tank upgrade menu.

How do I get gems?

Destroy other tanks — that is the official gem engine — plus 75,000 free gems from the NEWCURRENCY and HEADSTART codes. Details on the gems page.

How many tanks are there?

The official description says over 150 unique tanks. Build paths live in the in-game Index — no public tree is reliable. See the tanks page.

What badges exist?

Six official badges: destroy 100 and 10,000 of each food shape. Full list on the badges page.

Codes & rules

Boosts and bans.

The short version — with links to the full pages.

Which codes are official right now?

REBALANCEAGAIN (+250,000 XP), HAVEFUN (+200,000 XP), NEWCURRENCY (+50,000 gems), and HEADSTART (+25,000 gems) — printed on the official page, checked August 20, 2026. See the codes page.

How do I redeem codes?

From the lobby, press the blue tick / checkmark on the left side of the screen, enter the code exactly, and hit Redeem. The game does not pause while the menu is open — never redeem mid-fight.

Why do long code lists on other sites not match?

They are dated snapshots. Several still show dozens of older strings while missing codes the official page prints today, so this site mirrors only the official page.

What happens if I exploit?

The official description warns that all exploiters are permanently banned and an exploiting ban cannot be appealed. Do not test it.

Are there any gamepasses to buy?

No priced pass exists at this check — the catalog lists only an offsale pass named hello. Older articles quoting premium prices are outdated.

Community & lookalikes

Know the real game.

The mix-ups that send players to the wrong arena.

Is this the same as Tank Simulator?

No. This guide covers Tank Game by 7x3 (place 119789365111500). Other Roblox tank titles with similar names are different experiences — always check the developer name 7x3 before following a guide.

Is there a Discord?

Multiple guides point to the community Discord (discord.com/invite/WgCdbaMrrq) for new codes and announcements, alongside the 7x3 Roblox group.

Is the Fandom wiki official?

No. Treat fan wikis as unofficial community notes — never as the developer handbook. The in-game menus and the official game page win every disagreement.

What are the STARTER / PRO / MEGA places?

Trackers list sister places — STARTER, PRO, MEGA, TRADING, and Red VS Blue — alongside the main arena, but their matchmaking rules were not verified. Play the main place first.

Is this site official?

No. Tank Game Guide is an independent player resource, not affiliated with 7x3 or Roblox. Facts carry a check date, and the in-game screen always wins.
