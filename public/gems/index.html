Gems FAQ

Gem questions, answered.

Earning, spending, and the currency name confusion.

What is the fastest way to earn gems?

Redeem NEWCURRENCY and HEADSTART for 75,000 free gems, then destroy other tanks once your build wins trades. Until it does, food farming levels you toward builds that can fight — see the stats page.

Are gems and Diamonds the same thing?

Unproven. Official copy says gems; some community wikis say Diamonds for the shop currency. The in-game label is the authority — checked August 20, 2026.

Can I buy gems with Robux?

Not through a gamepass — the public catalog showed only an offsale pass named hello at this check. If the shop ever sells currency directly, the in-game store will say so.

What should I spend gems on first?

We do not print a shopping list: shop contents and prices are mutable and current guides disagree. Decide inside the game, after your class path is set.
